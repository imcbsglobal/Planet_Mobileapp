# Privacy Policy for MY IMC

**Last Updated:** January 2025

## Introduction

MY IMC ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### 1. Personal Information
- **User Credentials:** Email address and password for authentication
- **Profile Information:** Name, employee ID, profile picture
- **Contact Information:** Email address

### 2. Location Data
We collect precise location information when you:
- Punch in/out for attendance
- Start/end breaks
- Record work-related activities

**Purpose:** Location data is used to verify your attendance location and maintain accurate work records.

**Permission:** Location access is requested when you use attendance features and is only collected during active use of the app.

### 3. Camera and Photo Library Access
We request camera and photo library permissions for:
- Taking profile pictures
- Uploading images for work-related documentation
- Capturing photos for collection records, feeder management, and fuel tracking

**Purpose:** Camera access allows you to capture and upload images directly within the app for business purposes.

**Permission:** Camera access is requested only when you choose to take or upload a photo. We do not access your camera or photos without your explicit action.

### 4. Attendance and Work Data
- Punch in/out times and locations
- Break times and durations
- Working hours and attendance records
- Leave requests (early, late, and leave applications)
- Attendance verification status

### 5. Business Activity Data
- Collection records
- Feeder information
- Fuel tracking data
- Lead management information
- Target and performance data

### 6. Device Information
- Device type and model
- Operating system version
- App version
- Device identifiers for push notifications

### 7. Notification Data
- Push notification tokens
- Notification preferences
- Notification interaction history

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for:

1. **Authentication and Account Management**
   - Verifying your identity
   - Managing your account access
   - Maintaining secure login sessions

2. **Attendance Tracking**
   - Recording work hours
   - Verifying attendance locations
   - Calculating working hours and overtime
   - Managing break times

3. **Work Management**
   - Processing leave requests
   - Managing early/late arrival requests
   - Tracking business activities
   - Performance monitoring

4. **Communication**
   - Sending important notifications about attendance
   - Providing updates on leave request status
   - Delivering work-related announcements

5. **App Functionality**
   - Providing core features of the application
   - Improving user experience
   - Troubleshooting technical issues

## Data Storage and Security

### Storage
- User credentials are stored securely using device-level encryption (Expo SecureStore)
- Attendance and work data are stored on our secure servers (myimc.in)
- Local cache data is stored on your device for offline access

### Security Measures
- All data transmission uses HTTPS encryption
- Passwords are never stored in plain text
- Access to data is restricted to authorized personnel only
- Regular security audits and updates

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do NOT sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

1. **With Your Employer**
   - Attendance records
   - Work performance data
   - Leave request information
   - Business activity records

2. **Legal Requirements**
   - When required by law or legal process
   - To protect our rights and safety
   - To prevent fraud or security issues

3. **Service Providers**
   - Cloud hosting services (for data storage)
   - Push notification services
   - Analytics services (anonymized data only)

## Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

1. **Access Your Data:** Request a copy of your personal information
2. **Correct Your Data:** Update inaccurate or incomplete information
3. **Delete Your Data:** Request deletion of your account and associated data
4. **Opt-Out:** Disable location services or camera access in device settings
5. **Notification Control:** Manage notification preferences in the app

### How to Exercise Your Rights
Contact your system administrator or email us at support@myimc.in

## Location Services

**Android Permissions:**
- `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` - For precise location during attendance
- `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION` - For approximate location

**Control:** You can disable location services in your device settings, but this will prevent you from using attendance features.

## Camera and Storage Permissions

**Android Permissions:**
- `CAMERA` - For taking photos within the app
- `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` - For selecting photos from gallery
- `WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` - For saving photos (Android 9 and below)

**Control:** You can deny camera access, but you won't be able to take or upload photos within the app.

## Children's Privacy

Our app is intended for employees and is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Data Retention

- **Active Accounts:** Data is retained while your account is active
- **Inactive Accounts:** Data may be retained for up to 7 years for legal and business purposes
- **Deletion Requests:** Data will be deleted within 30 days of a valid deletion request

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

- Updating the "Last Updated" date
- Sending a notification for significant changes
